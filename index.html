⚔️ HOW OVERALL LEVELING WORKS
Individual Skills: Every level requires increasingly more XP. No skill has an easy shortcut.

Overall Levels 1–10: 1 individual skill level-up = 1 overall level.
Overall Levels 11–20: 2 individual skill level-ups = 1 overall level.
Overall Levels 21–30: 3 individual skill level-ups = 1 overall level.
Overall Levels 31–40: 4 individual skill level-ups = 1 overall level.

The number of skill level-ups required continues increasing every ten overall levels.
