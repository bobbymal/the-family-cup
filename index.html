📖 The Rules of Growth

  • Fresh start: JD and Michael begin at Overall Level 1, with all 28 skills at Level 1 and 0 XP.
  • Overall progression: Overall XP is earned once for an activity. It is not the sum of skill XP.
  • Primary skill: The main skill receives 100% of the activity's base XP.
  • Transfer skills: Related skills can receive a smaller share when the activity genuinely develops abilities useful in those skills.
  • Judgment matters: Not every skill receives XP from every activity. No artificial or unrelated XP.
  • Effort and time: Duration and effort influence XP. Quality and substance matter, not just minutes.
  • Permanent progress: Earned XP never decreases.
