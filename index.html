Every skill has its own XP and level. Higher levels require more XP, just like a RuneScape-style progression system.

Overall Level = the sum of all 28 skill levels.
Activities can legitimately improve multiple skills. Baseball, for example, can develop running, hand-eye coordination, golf swing mechanics, throwing, catching, decision-making, and other transferable abilities.

📜 THE GROWTH SYSTEM

  • Overall Level: Overall is the combined level of every individual skill.
  • Individual Skill XP: Every skill uses an escalating RuneScape-style XP curve.
  • XP Never Goes Backward: Once earned, skill XP remains earned.
  • Primary Skill: The activity receives the largest amount of XP in the skill it directly trains.
  • Transfer Skills: Activities can improve other skills when there is a legitimate connection.
  • Sports Transfer: Baseball can improve golf, running, football, basketball, soccer, bowling, strength, coordination, and decision-making.
  • Running: Running and speed can transfer to almost every sport where acceleration, sprinting, endurance, or movement is involved.
  • School: School develops reading, writing, mathematics, critical thinking, discipline, responsibility, and mental strength.
  • Spanish: Michael's dual-language work develops Spanish / Language.
  • Faith: Prayer, worship, church, Scripture, and spiritual growth contribute primarily to God Relationship, with legitimate secondary connections when appropriate.
  • Trust: Trust is separate from XP and can rise or fall based on choices. Skill XP is never erased.
Real growth happens one choice, one effort, and one level at a time.
