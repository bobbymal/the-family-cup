📊 ALL SKILLS

Every skill has its own XP and level. Higher levels require more XP, just like a RuneScape-style progression system.

Overall Level = the sum of all 28 skill levels. Activities can legitimately improve multiple skills. Baseball can improve running, golf swing mechanics, throwing, catching, coordination and decision-making. Running can transfer to almost every running-based sport.

📜 THE GROWTH SYSTEM

  • Overall Level: Overall is the combined level of all 28 individual skills.
  • Individual XP: Each skill follows an escalating RuneScape-style XP curve.
  • XP Never Goes Backward: Once XP is earned, it stays earned.
  • Primary Skill: The activity receives the largest amount of XP in the skill it directly develops.
  • Transfer Skills: Real abilities transfer between activities when there is a legitimate connection.
  • Baseball → Golf: Swing mechanics, timing, rotation, balance and hand-eye coordination can transfer from baseball to golf.
  • Running: Running, acceleration, speed and movement can transfer to baseball, soccer, football, basketball and other sports.
  • School: School develops reading, writing, mathematics, critical thinking, discipline, responsibility and mental strength.
  • Spanish: Michael's dual-language schoolwork develops Spanish / Language.
  • Faith: Prayer, worship, church and Scripture primarily develop God Relationship, with legitimate secondary connections.
  • Trust: Trust is separate from XP. Poor choices can affect Trust, but never erase skill XP.
Real growth happens one choice, one effort, and one level at a time.
