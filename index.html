Every skill has its own level and its own RuneScape-style XP curve. As a skill gets stronger, each new level requires more XP.

Overall Level = the combined level of every skill.

📜 HOW THE SYSTEM WORKS

  • Overall Level: Your Overall Level is the sum of every individual skill level.
  • Individual Skills: Every skill has its own level and XP. Each new level requires progressively more XP.
  • XP Never Decreases: Skill XP is permanent. A bad day does not erase progress.
  • Real Activities Matter: XP comes from actual effort, practice, learning, relationships, responsibility, character, and growth.
  • Transfer XP: An activity can help another skill when there is a legitimate connection. XP is never randomly spread everywhere.
  • Running: Running and speed can legitimately contribute to sports that require running.
  • School: School naturally contributes small amounts of XP to appropriate academic and character skills.
  • Spanish: Michael's dual-language school work contributes XP to Spanish / Language.
  • Trust: Trust is separate from XP. Poor choices can reduce Trust, but they do not erase skill progress.
  • Good Days: Trust can recover through consistent positive choices.
Real growth happens one choice, one effort, and one level at a time.
